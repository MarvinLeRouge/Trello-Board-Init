# Roadmap

No formal roadmap or committed timeline exists for this project yet. The ideas below are inferred from the "Known limitations" documented in the [README](../README.md) — they are potential directions, not confirmed plans.

## Potential improvement ideas

- **Per-card list targeting**: currently every card lands in `📥 Backlog` regardless of its intended status. The Markdown format could be extended to let each card specify its target list (e.g. `📅 This week`, `🔄 In progress`, `✅ Done`) instead of always defaulting to Backlog.

## Out of scope (platform constraints, not roadmap items)

Two other documented limitations are constraints of the Trello platform itself, not something this project can address:

- The Trello API rate limit (300 requests / 10 seconds).
- The Trello free plan's cap of 10 active boards per workspace.
